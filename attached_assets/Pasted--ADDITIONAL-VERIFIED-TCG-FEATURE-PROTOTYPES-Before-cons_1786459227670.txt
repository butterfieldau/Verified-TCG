## ADDITIONAL VERIFIED TCG FEATURE PROTOTYPES

Before considering the UI/UX prototype complete, I also want you to design and build polished clickable prototype screens for the features that will make Verified TCG stand out from normal card collection apps.

These features are mainly for VISUAL DESIGN + USER FLOW at this stage.

Use realistic mock data only.

Do NOT build complex backend systems, GPS infrastructure, live matching, messaging, payments or production algorithms yet.

### 1. TRADE MATCH

Create a dedicated Trade Match experience.

Show collectors where:

* they own cards I want
* I own cards they want

Example:

YOU WANT
Pikachu & Zekrom GX
PSA 10
$1,200 AUD

Charizard ex
TAG 10

Collector:

Make this feature feel exciting and important.

## IMPORTANT

These screens should feel integrated into the same Verified TCG design system.

Do NOT make them look like separate mini-apps.

Focus heavily on:

* premium mobile UI
* very clean information hierarchy
* card imagery
* matching indicators
* verification badges
* value comparisons
* simple interactions
* strong visual polish

Use mock data and clickable prototype flows only.

The goal is for me to be able to navigate through these features and understand exactly how Verified TCG could feel once the real backend is connected later.

Do NOT spend significant development effort on real matching logic or infrastructure yet.
