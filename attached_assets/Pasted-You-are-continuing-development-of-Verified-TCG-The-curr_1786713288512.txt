You are continuing development of **Verified TCG**.

The current app design and feature direction already exists. Do **not** redesign the entire app from scratch and do not remove existing features unless they are clearly broken, duplicated or unnecessary.

Your task is to now turn Verified TCG from a prototype/demo into a **fully functional, production-ready consumer app**.

The most important rule is:

> **If a user can see it, tap it, select it, edit it, search it, save it, delete it, share it or interact with it — it must actually work.**

There must be no fake buttons, dead links, placeholder actions, unfinished screens, demo-only functionality or UI elements that appear interactive but do nothing.

The target audience includes experienced collectors and complete beginners.

Do not assume users understand technical terminology.

The interface should feel:

* Fast
* Minimal

For every button/toggle/input/card/menu/link ask:

> "What happens when the user taps this?"

If the answer is:

"Nothing"

"Placeholder"

"Coming soon"

"Mock"

"Console log"

"Not connected yet"

then either complete it or intentionally disable/remove it until the functionality exists.

The final product should feel like a real App Store product, not a prototype.

Keep the app clean, intuitive and easy enough that a new collector understands how to use it without instructions.
